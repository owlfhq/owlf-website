Questions

A few things
to know.

Still have a question? Start with a message.

Do I need an owlf app?+

No separate app to install. Just start with a message in WhatsApp.

Can I request a service in my area?+

Tell us your city or neighbourhood in WhatsApp, and we’ll guide you through the options for your area.

What will my job cost?+

The price depends on the work. Make sure the price and what it includes are clear before you agree to the job.

What happens when I message owlf?+

You’ll be asked to describe the job and share your area. Follow the prompts in WhatsApp to continue your request.

What if my service isn’t listed?+

Send us a message describing the job and share your area. We’ll help you work out the next step.
