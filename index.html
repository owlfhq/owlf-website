Every provider on OWLF undergoes identity verification (government ID & background checks), trade certification validation, and customer rating audits before receiving requests.

Never. When our system matches nearby providers, they only see your approximate neighborhood (e.g. within 2-3 km). Your complete door address is only unlocked for the single specialist you personally choose to book.
